Numbers from past cases, knowledge from Synapt, the LLM only as judge.

Hybrid retrieval: Reciprocal Rank Fusion

Three ranked lists are merged by rank, not by score, so scores on different scales never need calibrating.

        Precedent estimates (no trained model)

        The Impact agent asks Synapt (trident_get_chunks) for the nearest past cases, then estimates each outcome as the median of the closest cohort of past cases, with the cohort's P10 and P90 as an 80% range. Cohort keys, most telling first: severity → size → tier → industry → backup → response-time band → strategy → type → region; keys are dropped from the end until at least 30 cases match. Synapt's precedents are shown with every estimate and cross-check it.

        Hold-out check (19,921 unseen cases)

        TargetR² / AUCMAEP10–P90 coverage

        Read honestly: production impact is predictable (R² 0.68). Revenue and recovery are noisier (R² 0.17–0.35), which is why every estimate shows a P10–P90 range and an "insufficient history" warning instead of a single number. The gradient-boosting models this replaced scored R² 0.70 / 0.33 / 0.40 / 0.27 and AUC 0.87; Synapt's nearest cases alone were less accurate (recovery MAE 35 d vs 25 d), so they are the evidence and the cross-check, not the number.

        What separates outcomes (share of variance, η²)

        Per incident, a driver is the cohort outcome minus the outcome of the same cohort with that attribute at its most common value; both sides keep the attribute and are matched on the same others (≥ 100 cases each).

        Agent contracts

        A Supervisor and eight goal-driven agents on a shared blackboard (LangGraph state). They are not if/else steps: every agent (agents/base.py) has a goal, a set of tools, and its own loop perceive → decide → act → observe until the goal is met; then it reflects (status, confidence) and posts a message that can ask another agent for work. The supervisor schedules by goal and dependency and acts on those requests. No LLM inside; every decision and every step is streamed to the console and saved with the report.

        Supervisor: goals × dependencies × requests

        agents/supervisor.py holds a goal table. Every round it 1) acts on agents' requests (within budgets), 2) keeps a fact-check backstop, 3) runs every open goal whose inputs are ready, in parallel, 4) finishes when all goals are met (hard stop: 25 decisions). Resetting an output re-opens exactly the goals that depend on it.

        Measured live on Synapt (ransomware incident, 11.8 s): 8 supervisor decisions; decision 2 started case search, the Synapt agent and the Impact agent together; 15 agent steps in total (Impact: ask Synapt for the nearest past cases → cohort estimate and cross-check → priority; Report: draft → fact-check → attach Synapt answer); judge APPROVE 90%. With a vague description ("a fire at one of our small suppliers") the Intake agent asked Synapt for labels, found the neighbours split (Electronics 43%, Asia-Pacific 34%) and left those fields unknown instead of guessing.

        The anti-hallucination chain

        1 · before

        Guardrails block injection and off-topic input, redact PII and validate fields against the dataset vocabulary.

        2 · during

        No generator LLM: the briefing is filled from facts, cases, strategy statistics and the Synapt playbook. Synapt's graph-RAG answer is shown as its own section with Synapt's quality signals.

        3 · after

        A deterministic fact-check rejects unknown case IDs, placeholders and amounts / percentages not in the evidence, The rule-based judge scores six criteria; the external LLM judges independently in evaluation.

        The feedback loop

        Rating

        1–5 stars + chosen strategy → strategy boost per (type, strategy), clamped to ×0.85–1.15 so a few votes cannot flip the ranking.

        Outcome

        Real recovery days and loss reported on the Incident Desk → added to the precedent pool on refresh, so the next estimate uses them.

        Synapt

        Every verdict is sent to trident_feedback; low-rated inputs are replayed as regression cases in the next evaluation.

        UI design

        Pages are grouped by the stage of the job, not by technology, so a non-expert can follow the story left to right. Every page has an ⓘ drawer that explains what it shows and how to read it.

        Plain language first

        Jargon terms are underlined; hovering shows a one-line glossary definition. Numbers come with their range and what they mean.

        Motion with meaning

        Animations show something real: agents lighting up as they finish, numbers counting to their value, flights following real taxi / take-off / landing phases.

        Light, calm palette

        One accent colour per stage; red is reserved for P1 and breaches. Works at phone width; the 3D view is lazy-loaded.
