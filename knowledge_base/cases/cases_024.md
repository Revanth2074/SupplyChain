Severity was 1 out of 5. Production impact was 13.7% (Low). Revenue loss was $138,546 (Under 1M).

Response strategy: Customer Delay, started after 2 days. Partial recovery took 22 days and full recovery took 45 days (Normal). The supplier relationship was kept.

Assessment: priority P4 - Low (score 11.8/100; action SLA: Monitor weekly). Main drivers: low severity (1 of 5); no backup supplier (recoveries without one take about 26 days longer on average); a fast response start (2 days).

Outcome: full recovery took 45 days versus a median of 32 days for 461 similar Natural Disaster disruptions (severity 1, without a backup supplier): 13 days slower than typical, so the Customer Delay response under-performed.

Recommended playbook: SOP-005 (Respond to a Natural Disaster disruption).
