Severity was 4 out of 5. Production impact was 31.3% (Moderate). Revenue loss was $1,413,078 (1M to 5M).

Response strategy: Production Reroute, started after 17 days. Partial recovery took 51 days and full recovery took 114 days (Slow). The supplier was permanently replaced.

Assessment: priority P2 - High (score 57.7/100; action SLA: Response plan within 24h). Main drivers: high severity (4 of 5); no backup supplier (recoveries without one take about 26 days longer on average); a slow response start (17 days); a deep-tier supplier (Tier 3) with less visibility.

Outcome: full recovery took 114 days versus a median of 126 days for 532 similar Factory Incident disruptions (severity 4, without a backup supplier): in line with similar cases.

Recommended playbook: SOP-002 (Respond to a Factory Incident disruption).
