# Disruption Response Playbooks

Standard operating procedures derived from the outcomes of 100000 historical supply chain disruptions. Each playbook lists the response strategies in order of fastest average full recovery.

## Procedure: Respond to a Cyber Attack disruption

Intent: restore production after a Cyber Attack disruption with the lowest recovery time and loss.

1. Classify the incident: confirm it is a Cyber Attack disruption and record industry, supplier tier, supplier region, supplier size, backup supplier status and severity (1-5).
2. Assess impact: historical Cyber Attack disruptions averaged 41.3% production impact and $3,365,434 revenue loss; severity 4-5 cases should be triaged as P1 or P2.
3. Check backup supplier: cases with a backup recovered fully in 84.3 days on average versus 118.7 days without one. Activate the backup supplier if one exists.
4. Start the response within 3 days: responses started within 3 days averaged 93.9 days to full recovery versus 113.5 days when started after 7 days.
5. Select the response strategy, preferring the fastest historical option: Inventory Buffer (80.8 days average full recovery), then Alternative Supplier (81.5 days). Avoid Combined Strategy unless constrained (125.2 days).
6. Track recovery: expect partial recovery in about 36 days; escalate if full recovery exceeds 149 days.
7. Review the supplier: 29% of Cyber Attack disruptions ended in a permanent supplier change; record the outcome and feedback for continuous improvement.
